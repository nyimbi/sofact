## Precis

A commercial software-factory product ("droids") that positions itself as the
current wave of the software factory: general-purpose models plus domain
scaffolding, sold to enterprises as an engineering-capacity multiplier. Useful
primarily as *market evidence* — that the concept has buyers and that the
competitive framing is "assemble the line, don't sell a smarter model".

## Key claims (as positioning)

- Enterprise buyers want the production line, not the model.
- Integration into existing repos/CI is the moat, not model quality.

## Why it matters for sofact

Two uses. (1) Market: confirms a market exists above the model layer. (2) Risk:
the obvious commercial competitors are selling the *organisational* version of
this to enterprises, so a single-operator local factory must differentiate on
something they structurally cannot offer — full local data sovereignty, zero
marginal token cost, and the ability to run unattended on owned hardware.
