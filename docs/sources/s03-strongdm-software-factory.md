## Precis

StrongDM's manifesto-style argument that the software factory is the correct
organisational unit for AI-era engineering: specifications in, verified software
out, with humans owning intent and acceptance rather than implementation. It is
product-positioned (vendor with a stake in the answer) but it names the right
primitives: intent capture as a first-class artifact, machine-checkable
acceptance criteria, and a production line with queues and gates rather than a
chat loop.

## Key claims

- The unit of work is a *specification*, not a prompt or a ticket.
- Acceptance criteria must be machine-checkable to be automatable.
- Humans belong at intent and acceptance; the middle is the factory's job.

## Why it matters for sofact

Frames the deliverable shape: sofact should emit specification artifacts
(versioned, reviewable) rather than a stream of agent actions. Also the clearest
statement of the division of labour the spec must encode.
