## Precis

Revives the long-dormant HTTP `402 Payment Required` status code as a real
settlement mechanism. A server returns `402` with payment requirements; a client
(including an autonomous agent) pays and retries with proof. Designed for
machine-to-machine micropayments — sub-cent, per-request pricing without accounts,
API keys, or invoicing. Settlement is on-chain (stablecoins) with a facilitator
role to keep the client side simple.

## Key claims

- Payments can be made a *protocol primitive* rather than a billing integration,
  which is what makes per-request agent-to-agent commerce tractable.
- Removing account creation removes the main friction for autonomous clients.

## Why it matters for sofact

Turns "monetize" from a hand-wave into an interface: sofact-produced services can
expose metered endpoints that agents can pay for without human onboarding. This
is the most concrete monetisation path encountered, and the one that matches a
machine-consumer customer base rather than a human one.

## Concerns / information quality

- Vendor protocol with a commercial interest in adoption (Coinbase as
  facilitator). Standardisation status unclear.
- Crypto-settlement/regulatory exposure varies by jurisdiction; "monetize
  autonomously" and "handle payments autonomously" are different risk classes.
- The monetisation question for sofact is upstream of the payment rail anyway:
  *what* is worth paying for is a product question, not a protocol question.
